# 🧬 SoulSpecies — Biometric Creature & Spirit Identity Lab

A creative, aesthetic, and hilarious web experience where users scan their face, answer funny personality questions across multiple life topics, and discover what non-human creature, animal, or everyday object they would be in this present life!

## ✨ Features

- **📸 Biometric Quantum Face Scanner**:
  - Live webcam access with cyber-HUD reticle, sweeping animated laser, and live funny telemetry readouts (*"Nap Urgency: 98% (Critical)"*, *"Patience Buffer: 404 Not Found"*).
  - Built-in photo upload option and demo cartoon selfie for users without a camera.
- **🎨 Animated Cartoons & Doodles**:
  - 15 expressive, custom animated SVG cartoons with CSS animations (e.g. Chaos Goose with bobbing knife, Sloth Bear swaying with floating 'Zzz' doodles, Screaming Espresso Machine with vibrating pressure gauge & steam puffs, Zen Capybara soaking in hot water with a floating citrus, and many more).
  - Floating aesthetic background doodles (stars, smiling coffee cups, ghosties, cartoon eyes, planets).
- **💡 16 Multi-Topic Funny Personality Questions**:
  - Covers social battery, panic/stress deadlines, Wi-Fi outages, 2 AM snack raids, attention spans, texting speeds, alarms, grocery runs, suitcase packing, handling criticism, room cleaning, group dinner decisions, workouts, rainy days, Wikipedia rabbit holes, and board games!
  - Choose between **Quick Scan (6 Questions)** or **Full Soul Deep-Dive (All 16 Questions)**.
- **🪪 Official Spirit Identity Passport**:
  - Side-by-side comparison of the user's biometric face scan and their true spirit form.
  - Genetic & Spiritual match percentage (e.g., *98.5% Match*).
  - Funny scientific Latin classification, personality roast, famous quote, and custom spirit attribute meters (Chaos Energy, Nap Requirement, Snack Urgency, Drama Factor, Patience Buffer).
  - **Download as PNG**: Generates a high-res, printable Intergalactic Spirit Passport card directly on HTML5 Canvas.
- **🔊 Built-in Web Audio Synthesizer**:
  - Zero external MP3/audio files needed — synthesized laser scanner, camera clicks, UI chirps, and creature greetings generated in real-time.
- **📖 Complete Creature Codex**:
  - Browse all 15 creatures, hear their voice sound effects, and read their roasts anytime.

## 🚀 How to Run

1. Open File Explorer to:
   `C:\Users\user\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\creature-morph/`
2. Double-click **`index.html`** to open it directly in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Firefox.
3. Allow camera access (or click "Use Demo Cartoon Face" / "Upload Photo").
4. Answer the funny questions and download your Official Spirit Identity Card!
